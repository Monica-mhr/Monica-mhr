# Hi, I'm Monica M. Hernandez 👋
**System Designer, PMP |Founder and technical architect, BC-DS**

> *"Distortion doesn't begin in meetings. It begins the moment one person frames a decision before they've finished thinking, and AI is now present at exactly that moment. The SDA was built to govern that boundary."*

With over 25 years at the intersection of strategy and execution, I work as the operational architect of Business Consultants for Digital Solutions, LLC (BC-DS) and as a framework co-author. I specialize in turning decision-making philosophy into operationally sound infrastructure.
